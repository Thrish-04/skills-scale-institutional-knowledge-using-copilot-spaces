# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers research user needs and shape the interaction and visual design of product experiences. They make usability a visible part of product decisions without replacing the Product Manager's ownership of product outcomes and priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research and usability validation
- Create and maintain user flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Identify accessibility and usability needs and incorporate them into designs
- Clarify experience-related acceptance criteria and design decisions

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user research to product outcomes and priorities
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and ensure implementation follows the design intent
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility in validation
- Share research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager and relevant Stakeholders

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound engineering decisions. They own technical design recommendations and communicate trade-offs; the Product Manager retains product scope and priority decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design for the initiative
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Facilitate engineering trade-off decisions and document significant decisions
- Support Developers with technical direction and maintainable implementation approaches

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, estimates, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Explain feasibility, options, and technical risks to the Product Manager to inform scope and priority decisions
- Coordinate dependencies and technical milestones with the Project Manager
- Work with QA/Testing on testability and integration risks, and with Stakeholders when technical decisions affect them

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers help ensure services can be deployed, operated, and supported reliably. They advise on operational readiness and reliability; delivery and product priorities remain aligned with the Project Manager and Product Manager.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on infrastructure, deployment automation, and environment needs
- Define or improve monitoring, alerting, and reliability measures
- Identify operational risks and contribute mitigation and recovery plans
- Support release readiness and post-deployment verification

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to make services deployable, observable, and operable
- Coordinate test environments and operational verification with QA/Testing
- Work with the Project Manager on release dependencies, readiness tracking, and escalation of operational risks
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders about reliability trade-offs and operational impact

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define how outcomes will be measured and ensure the data used for decisions is fit for purpose. The Product Manager owns product success metrics; this role advises on their measurement and reporting.

### Responsibilities
- Translate agreed success metrics into instrumentation and reporting needs
- Advise on event definitions, data quality, and interpretation
- Identify gaps or risks in the availability and reliability of measurement data
- Help teams evaluate results after delivery and communicate evidence

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to make success metrics measurable and useful for product decisions
- Coordinate instrumentation and data access needs with Developers
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate event capture and data quality
- Align reporting milestones and communicate measurement risks with the Project Manager and relevant Stakeholders

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads advise teams on security, privacy, and applicable compliance considerations. They identify requirements and risks and recommend controls; implementation ownership is agreed with the delivery team and relevant accountable stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements, risks, and review checkpoints
- Advise on appropriate safeguards, data handling, and access controls
- Coordinate security or privacy assessments and communicate findings
- Recommend mitigations and escalation when material risks remain

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to include security and privacy needs in product scope and acceptance criteria
- Partner with Developers on secure implementation and with QA/Testing on validation
- Coordinate review timing and risk tracking with the Project Manager
- Explain relevant risks, decisions, and mitigations to Stakeholders

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Operations Representatives bring customer-impact and service-readiness perspectives to planning and delivery. They help prepare support teams and handoffs without replacing the Product Manager's ownership of product direction or the Project Manager's delivery coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer issues, support needs, and operational constraints
- Identify documentation, training, and escalation needs before release
- Help define support readiness and communicate known issues or customer impact
- Provide feedback on post-release questions and operational outcomes

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Share customer needs and feedback with the Product Manager to inform priorities and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate readiness tasks, stakeholder communications, and handoffs with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to understand known issues, validate support scenarios, and prepare troubleshooting information
- Coordinate release information and escalation paths with DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers and relevant Stakeholders
